URBANWEAR - FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
(Demo store. All information is fictional.)

ORDERS AND PAYMENT

Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept cash on delivery.

Q: Can I cancel or change my order?
A: You can cancel or change your order within 1 hour of placing it. Contact support@urbanwear.example with your order number as soon as possible. After 1 hour, the order goes to our warehouse and we cannot guarantee changes. If the order has already shipped, please wait to receive it and use our return process.

Q: How do I know my order was placed successfully?
A: You will receive an order confirmation email within a few minutes. If you do not see it, check your spam folder or contact us.

Q: Do you have a physical store?
A: No, UrbanWear is an online-only store.

DISCOUNTS

Q: Do you have a discount code?
A: New customers get 10% off their first order with the code WELCOME10. The code works once per customer, cannot be combined with other offers, and does not apply to Final Sale items.

Q: Can I use two discount codes on one order?
A: No, only one discount code can be used per order.

Q: Do you offer student or military discounts?
A: Not at the moment.

SHIPPING AND TRACKING

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Standard shipping in the US takes 3-5 business days. Express takes 1-2 business days. International orders take 7-14 business days. Orders are processed in 1-2 business days before shipping.

Q: Is shipping free?
A: US orders over $75 get free standard shipping. Otherwise standard shipping is $4.99.

Q: Where is my order?
A: We email a tracking number within 24 hours after your order ships. Use the link in that email, or the Track Order page on our website with your order number and email.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, to Canada, the UK, the EU, and Australia. Shipping starts at $14.99 and customers pay any import duties.

RETURNS AND EXCHANGES

Q: What is your return policy?
A: You can return most items within 30 days of delivery if they are unworn, unwashed, and have tags attached. Returns in the US are free.

Q: How long does a refund take?
A: Refunds are issued within 5-7 business days after we receive your return, to the original payment method.

Q: Can I exchange for a different size?
A: Yes, exchanges are free, subject to stock availability.

Q: Which items cannot be returned?
A: Final Sale items, underwear, socks, swimwear, and gift cards.

Q: My item arrived damaged. What should I do?
A: Email support@urbanwear.example within 7 days with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or refund you fully, including shipping.

PRODUCTS AND CARE

Q: How do I choose my size?
A: See our Size Guide. If you are between sizes, we recommend the larger size for regular-fit items. Contact us with your height and weight if you need help.

Q: Are your clothes sustainable?
A: Our Classic Cotton Tee uses organic cotton, and our Rain Jacket and Puffer Vest use recycled polyester. Not all products are made from sustainable materials.

Q: Will the linen dress shrink?
A: Linen can shrink slightly in the first wash. Wash cold and hang dry to reduce shrinking.

CONTACT

Q: How can I contact customer support?
A: Email support@urbanwear.example. Our team is available Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST, and replies within 24 hours on business days.

Q: Do you have phone support?
A: No, we currently offer email support only.
