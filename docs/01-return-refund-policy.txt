URBANWEAR - RETURN AND REFUND POLICY
(Demo store. All information is fictional.)

1. Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of the delivery date.

2. Condition of returned items
Items must be unworn, unwashed, and have the original tags attached. Items that show signs of wear, washing, perfume, or pet hair cannot be accepted.

3. Items that cannot be returned
- Items marked "Final Sale" on the product page
- Underwear, socks, and swimwear (for hygiene reasons)
- Gift cards
- Items returned after 30 days

4. How to start a return
Step 1: Go to the Returns page on our website and enter your order number and email address.
Step 2: Select the items you want to return and choose a reason.
Step 3: Print the prepaid return label (or show the QR code at a drop-off point).
Step 4: Pack the items and drop the parcel at the carrier location shown on the label.

5. Return shipping cost
Returns within the United States are free. For international orders, the customer pays return shipping.

6. Refunds
Once we receive and inspect your return, we will email you. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive the parcel. Your bank may take an extra 3-5 business days to show the money. Original shipping charges are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

7. Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or color for free, subject to stock availability. If the new item is not in stock, we will refund you instead.

8. Damaged, defective, or wrong items
If your item arrives damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered, contact support@urbanwear.example within 7 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will send a replacement or give a full refund, including shipping, at no cost to you.

9. Late or missing refunds
If you have not received your refund after 10 business days, contact support@urbanwear.example with your order number.
