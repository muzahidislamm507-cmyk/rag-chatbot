URBANWEAR - SHIPPING POLICY
(Demo store. All information is fictional.)

1. Order processing
Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders placed before 2:00 PM EST on a business day are usually shipped the same or next business day. Orders placed on weekends or holidays are processed the next business day.

2. Shipping options and costs (United States)
- Standard shipping: 3-5 business days, $4.99. Free for orders over $75.
- Express shipping: 1-2 business days, $12.99.
We do not ship to PO boxes with Express shipping.

3. International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and Australia.
- Delivery time: 7-14 business days
- Cost: starts at $14.99, calculated at checkout based on weight and destination
Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their country.

4. Order tracking
You will receive an email with a tracking number within 24 hours after your order ships. You can track your parcel using the link in that email or on the "Track Order" page of our website using your order number and email.

5. Delays
Delivery times are estimates. Delays can happen during holidays, sales events, or bad weather. If your parcel has not arrived 3 business days after the estimated delivery date, contact support@urbanwear.example.

6. Wrong address
Please check your address carefully at checkout. If you notice a mistake, contact us within 1 hour of ordering. Once an order has shipped, we cannot change the address. Parcels returned to us because of a wrong address can be re-sent after the customer pays the shipping cost again.

7. Lost parcels
If the tracking shows "delivered" but you did not receive your parcel, check with neighbors and household members first, then contact us within 5 days. We will open an investigation with the carrier.
