FIT NOTES
- The Classic Cotton Tee and Everyday Hoodie have a regular fit. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size.
- The Slim Fit Jeans run slim. We recommend sizing up if you prefer a relaxed fit.
- The Oversized Hoodie is designed to be loose. Choose your usual size for the intended look, or size down for a less oversized fit.
- The Linen Summer Dress runs true to size.

HOW TO MEASURE
Chest/bust: measure around the fullest part of your chest.
Waist: measure around the narrowest part of your waist.
If you are unsure, contact support@urbanwear.example with your height and weight and we will suggest a size.
